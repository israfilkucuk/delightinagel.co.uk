Implant design consultancy

Creating, analysing and developing implantable & functionalised drug-elucidated implant designs

Delightinagel Ltd supports clients developing new intraocular lenses and other ophthalmic implants with novel surface features. Every new design is monitored through non-clinical safety testing and assessed against ISO 10993:2018, ISO 11979:2020, MHRA guidance and the EU Commission's Article 117 framework for drug-device combination products — the groundwork needed for regulatory approval to move into animal and human studies.

Ongoing support covers material attributes, process capability and implant surface functionalisation methods, keeping the experimental work behind a premarket submission report accurate throughout.

Bench & biocompatibility testing

Project-specific non-clinical testing of medical implants

Preliminary laboratory testing across mechanical, physical, chemical, thermal and biological properties, alongside consultancy during test report preparation and assessment. Reports are kept current with updates from MHRA and FDA, and client representatives or third parties are welcome to accompany testing where needed.
